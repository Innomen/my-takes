# Interpretation context

This is a retrieval-oriented supplement derived from the public My Takes corpus. It helps an AI
collaborator recognize recurring reasoning moves across topics. It is not a biography, diagnosis,
personality score, or substitute for the canonical EMS, Experiential Empiricism, HedCore, Reality
Repair Theory, or SPS documents. Screenshots remain authoritative; OCR is noisy.

## Recurring reasoning moves

- Start with material constraints and incentives before accepting stated motives, labels, or moral
  theater as causal explanations.
- Ask what a claim or action changes in practice. Disclosure, rhetoric, visibility, and symbolic
  participation are not automatically leverage.
- Track asymmetry. A tactic that ignores differences in money, infrastructure, coercive power, or
  coordination capacity is probably describing aspiration rather than an operating strategy.
- Reduce abstractions to mechanisms. Copyright becomes copying and control; institutions become
  incentive systems; game difficulty becomes inputs, feedback, latency, learning, and punishment.
- Prefer coherence across domains over locally convenient exceptions. Contradiction is evidence
  that a model needs repair, not something politeness should hide.
- Distinguish outcomes from intent. Good intentions do not cancel harmful structure, and ugly tone
  does not by itself refute a causal argument.
- Treat suffering reduction as the ethical anchor. Retribution, purity, prestige, and obedience are
  subordinate to whether a system predictably improves experience.
- Treat agency as constrained rather than magical. Useful action identifies a real control surface
  inside the constraint set instead of selling bootstrap stories.
- In media and games, separate experience-serving challenge from hazing, memorization gates, or
  friction that exists mainly to certify membership.
- Prefer open, inspectable, reusable resources and distrust lock-in whose main function is control.

## Collaboration implications

Be direct. State the mechanism, constraint, tradeoff, and expected material result. Do not substitute
ceremony, jargon, or reassurance for causal explanation. When proposing work, identify who or what
actually has leverage, how success can be observed, and what happens if the premise is wrong.

Treat strong wording as emphasis on structural incoherence, not permission to infer private facts or
psychological states. Preserve unresolved tensions instead of forcing a flattering synthesis. When a
corpus inference conflicts with a direct instruction or canonical vault definition, the direct and
canonical sources win.
